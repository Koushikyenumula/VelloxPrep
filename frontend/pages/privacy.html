Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

At VelloxPrep, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our AI-driven interview preparation platform.

1. Information Collection

We collect several different types of information to provide and improve our Service to you:

  • Personal Data: While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you, such as your email address, first name, last name, and professional background.
  • Usage Data: We automatically collect information on how the Service is accessed and used. This includes your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, browser version, the pages of our Service that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and diagnostic data.
  • Assessment Data: As an interview preparation platform, we collect the audio, video, text, and code inputs you provide during practice sessions. This data is processed by our AI engines to generate scoring and feedback.

2. Use of Data

VelloxPrep uses the collected data for various professional purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and monitor the usage of our Service.
  • To generate personalized interview feedback, scoring, and telemetry based on your inputs.
  • To notify you about changes to our Service and provide customer support.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or security vulnerabilities.

3. AI Processing and Data Handling

Your assessment data (including voice transcripts and coding inputs) is processed utilizing advanced AI models. We implement strict data anonymization protocols where applicable. We do not use your personal interview data to train public foundation models without your explicit, opt-in consent.

4. Data Retention and Security

We will retain your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. We deploy enterprise-grade encryption and secure architecture to protect your data. However, remember that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Data Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data stored on our servers. You may export your interview dossiers or permanently delete your account and associated telemetry at any time through your Profile dashboard.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
